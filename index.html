About

I am a Staff Data Scientist at Walmart Global Tech in Sunnyvale, CA. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Virginia Tech in 2024, where I worked with Dr. Sharath Raghvendra in the Algorithms Lab. My research focuses on optimal transport and combinatorial algorithms for machine learning. Before that, I earned an M.S. in Mining and Mineral Engineering from Virginia Tech in 2019, advised by Dr. Bahareh Nojabaei, where I focused on computational simulation of multiphase flow in complex reservoir systems.

My work has been published in leading machine learning venues, including ICML, NeurIPS, and ICLR.
